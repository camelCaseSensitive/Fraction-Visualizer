Single Fraction Visualizer

Enter one fraction and choose how to visualize it.

Enter to begin

Supports improper fractions. Press ? for controls.

Controls

2–9
multiply the fraction by n/n
Shift + 2–9
divide the fraction by n/n when possible
V
toggle between circle slices and fraction bars
C
switch to circle slices
B
switch to fraction bars
Z
undo the last change
R
reset to the starting fraction
F
choose a new fraction
? or Esc
close this help

Example: tap 3 to turn 1/2 into 3/6. Tap Shift + 3 to turn 3/6 back into 1/2.
